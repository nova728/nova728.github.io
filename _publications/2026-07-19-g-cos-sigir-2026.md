## Overview

Generative information retrieval systems answer a query with synthesized text rather than a ranked
list of documents, which makes classic relevance-based metrics a poor proxy for how satisfied a user
actually is. **G-CoS** frames satisfaction as an explicit trade-off between two interpretable terms:
the **gain** a generated response delivers with respect to the user's information need, and the
**cost** the user pays — in reading effort, redundancy, and verification — to extract that gain.
Because the two terms are estimated separately, the resulting score can be decomposed and inspected
rather than treated as an opaque number.
